YOUMUSE PRODUCT CATALOGUE — PUBLISHING GUIDE

CURRENT DISPLAY
- All 19 hat category pages use one shared dynamic catalogue script.
- No fake or blank product cards are shown. Empty categories show a short empty-state message.
- Desktop: 4 columns x up to 4 rows (16 products per page).
- Tablet: 2 columns. Mobile: 2 columns.
- Pagination appears only when a category has more than 16 published products.
- Search and filters operate on the published products in the selected category.

HOW TO PUBLISH A PRODUCT ON THIS STATIC WEBSITE
1. Add the product image under v13work/assets/products/ (WebP recommended).
2. Open v13work/data/products.json.
3. Add one product object to the array for its category.
4. Set status to "published" to make it visible. Use "draft" to keep it hidden.
5. Run: python scripts/build-product-catalogue.py
6. Upload/commit the changed JSON, generated product pages, sitemap.xml and image files with the website.

IMPORTANT
- Product image paths in products.json are relative to the website root (v13work/).
- Every product slug must be unique within its category.
- Only records with status "published", a slug and a name are displayed.
- Each category is independent; products are never mixed between categories.
- This is a static-site publishing workflow, not a login-based product CMS. Products are published by updating products.json and image files, then running scripts/build-product-catalogue.py. The script generates SEO-friendly static product detail pages and updates sitemap.xml automatically.
