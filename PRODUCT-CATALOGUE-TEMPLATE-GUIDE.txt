CAPVORA PRODUCT CATALOGUE TEMPLATE — V8

What is included
- 19 independent hat-category directory pages under /hat-styles/.
- 16 blank product cards per category (4 columns x 4 rows on desktop).
- 304 individual product detail pages under /products/<category>/product-01.html ... product-16.html.
- Every category page has a left sidebar with all 19 categories plus logo-technique, material and application filters.
- Each product card has a Read More → link to its own unique product detail page.
- Each product detail page has blank image areas and editable sections:
  Specifications, Description, Product Attributes, Customization, Packing & Shipping.
- Black Request a Quote and Online Design buttons reuse the existing site quote modal.
- Existing Capvora logo, header, footer, contact rail, WhatsApp/Email/Facebook links, navigation and black/white/grey visual theme are retained.

No product hats or product photos were generated. All product image areas are neutral placeholders for the owner's own photos.

How to add products later
1. Open the relevant /hat-styles/<category>.html page.
2. Find a card titled "Product Name — Item 01".
3. Replace the placeholder image area with your own image, for example:
   <img src="../../assets/products/baseball-caps/product-01.webp" alt="Your product name">
   (For category listing pages, use ../assets/... or place images in /assets.)
4. Replace the product name and short description.
5. Open the matching detail page under /products/<category>/product-01.html.
6. Replace the main image and thumbnail placeholders; fill in the real description and confirmed specifications.
7. Add the product's actual filter attributes to its card's data-technique, data-material and data-scene values, separated by | when multiple values apply.

Filter controls are wired, but placeholder cards intentionally have no assigned product attributes yet. They become meaningful as real product metadata is added.

Layout
- Desktop: 4 columns x 4 rows (16 product slots per category page).
- Tablet: 3 columns.
- Mobile: 2 columns; very narrow screens: 1 column.

Important
