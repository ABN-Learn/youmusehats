CAPVORA V10 PREVIEW — HOW TO OPEN

1. Download and extract Capvora-V10-Homepage-and-Product-Catalogue-Preview.zip.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another modern browser to preview the homepage.
3. On the homepage, find "Explore Our Hat Styles":
   - Desktop: 5 cards visible at a time; 10 cards total across 2 slides.
   - Mobile: 2 cards visible at a time; swipe horizontally or use the arrows.
   - Both arrows and cards retain their complete borders; no autoplay.
4. Click "Explore More Hat Styles" to open all-hat-styles.html:
   - All 19 current hat categories are displayed in a responsive 4/3/2-column grid.
   - Use the search box to find a hat category.
   - The grid is not limited to 19; additional category cards can be added later.
5. Click a category (for example Baseball Caps):
   - Each category page contains 16 products of that same hat type.
   - Craft/logo technique, materials, color, and application filters remain in place.
6. Click "Read More" under a product to open its individual detail page.

WHAT CHANGED IN V10
- Homepage carousel changed from 8 cards / 4 visible on desktop to 10 cards / 5 visible on desktop.
- The carousel calculates card widths from the available viewport width to keep every card border fully visible.
- Mobile shows 2 cards per slide and supports native touch swipe.
- All Hat Styles page was simplified: redundant information panels and factory-tour block removed.
- All Hat Styles now has a compact heading, category search, and all 19 category cards.
- Existing black/white/gray theme, orange accents, black quote button, site navigation and contact links retained.
- Existing 19 category pages and 304 product detail templates retained unchanged.
- Each category still has 16 same-type product slots and its existing filters.
- No new hat images were generated; existing approved site images were reused.

This is a local preview package only. It has not been deployed to the live domain.
