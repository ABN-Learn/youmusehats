Why Choose Custom Baseball Caps?

Baseball caps offer a familiar silhouette and a visible area for branding. They are used in fashion collections, company merchandise, events and team apparel. The right construction depends on your audience and intended use.

Five-Panel and Six-Panel Caps

Six-panel caps have a classic construction with multiple seams. Five-panel options create a different front profile that may suit certain artwork. The best choice depends on the desired look and logo size.

Structured and Unstructured Designs

Structured caps have a firmer front shape; unstructured caps feel softer and more relaxed. Curved or flatter brims and different closures further change the appearance and fit.

Choose the Fabric

Cotton is popular for casual headwear. Polyester is often selected for lightweight or performance-oriented styles. Blends and specialty fabrics offer other textures and functional properties. Ask about availability for your desired color and quantity.

Logo Decoration Options

Flat embroidery is versatile for many brand marks. 3D puff embroidery creates a raised look for bold lettering. Printing and woven or leather patches provide alternative finishes. Review the artwork, fabric and logo position before choosing.

Private-Label Details

Branded woven labels, hang tags, custom packaging and selected closure details can help create a cohesive collection. Confirm these requirements in the quote because they affect cost and production planning.

Small-Batch Orders and Delivery
