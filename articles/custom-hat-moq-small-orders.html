What Is MOQ?

MOQ means minimum order quantity. Requirements vary with hat construction, available materials and decoration processes. YOUMUSE welcomes small-batch custom hat inquiries, including single-sample requests. Exact options and pricing are reviewed for each design.

Why Start with a Small Order?

Small runs help clothing brands test demand, review fit and embroidery, prepare promotional samples and reduce initial inventory commitments. A sample also gives buyers an opportunity to confirm the logo position and overall appearance before a larger run.

What Affects Small-Batch Pricing?

Hat style, fabric, color, logo size, embroidery or patch technique, the number of decoration positions, packaging and quantity all affect the quotation. Smaller orders may cost more per piece because setup work is spread over fewer units. Larger quantities may qualify for a lower unit price.

Production and Delivery

Standard custom production typically takes around seven days after the relevant specifications and approvals are confirmed. Complex designs or larger quantities may take longer. Shipping from China is separate; eligible express routes may offer an estimated 3–5 day transit time after dispatch, not a guaranteed arrival date.

How to Request a Quote

Send the hat style, desired quantity, logo file, preferred decoration technique, colors and destination. Submit artwork on the website, by email or through WhatsApp. The team will review the details and quote the order and freight individually.

Practical advice for buyers

Check what the minimum applies to

Ask whether the minimum applies to the whole order, each hat style, each colour or each logo. An order of 100 spread across four colours may use four fabric and decoration setups. Give the supplier your exact split and identify where you can consolidate.

Use the first run to learn

Start with one silhouette and a small number of colour and logo combinations. Decide what you want to learn, such as which colour sells first or whether the fit works across your intended audience. Track the results by variation and note the units available for each.

Compare first and repeat runs

Ask for a breakdown of sample, setup, unit price, packing and freight. Confirm what can be reused on a repeat run, which materials are available and whether adding a colour changes the minimum. Keep the approved sample as your reorder reference.

Common buyer question

Does a small order have the same unit price as a repeat?

Ask for prices at both quantities. Setup, fabric availability and packaging can make the first run and a repeat differ.

Common Small-Order Problems and How to Resolve Them

My total quantity meets the minimum, but each color does not

Send a breakdown such as 100 caps: 60 navy and 40 beige, with the same front logo. Ask whether both colors can share the same fabric type, closure and decoration setup. If a material minimum applies separately, compare using an available stock color with increasing the quantity. Do not assume that the total order quantity satisfies every material requirement.

I want a custom fabric color for a short run

Custom dyeing may involve a separate fabric minimum and an approval stage. Ask for an available-color alternative alongside the custom-dyed option. Compare the actual swatch, total project cost and schedule before choosing; a screen color is not a physical color approval.

Can I change the logo on every cap?

Individual names or different artwork can introduce additional preparation and checking. Provide the complete variation list before requesting a price. Ask which parts of the design remain shared and how each variation affects production, packing and error checking.

The unit price is low, but delivery makes the order expensive

Compare the full delivered budget for the same destination, not just the factory unit price. Request freight separately and confirm the shipment dimensions, packing method and delivery terms. Adding units may reduce some costs per hat while increasing your total cash commitment, so compare both totals before deciding.

How can I make a repeat order easier?

Keep the approved sample reference, fabric specification, measurements, artwork version, thread colors and packaging instructions. Ask whether the same materials are still available before promising customers an identical repeat. A previous order does not automatically reserve fabric or production time.
