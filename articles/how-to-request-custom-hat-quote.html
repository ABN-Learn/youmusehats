Why Quotes Vary

Custom hats are priced according to specifications, not just the number of units. Cap construction, fabric, colors, logo complexity, decoration technique, packaging and order quantity can all change the cost.

Choose Your Hat Style

Specify whether you need baseball caps, dad caps, trucker hats, bucket hats, golf caps or beanies. A reference photo can help when you do not know the style name.

Send Your Logo File

Attach an AI, EPS, SVG, PDF or high-resolution PNG file where available. Files can be submitted on the website, by email or via WhatsApp. Include the preferred logo size and front, side or back placement.

Specify Decoration and Quantity

Tell the team whether you prefer flat embroidery, 3D puff embroidery, printing or a patch. State the quantity you want quoted; you can also ask for comparisons at 50, 100, 500 or 1,000 units. Small-batch inquiries and samples are welcome.

Share Materials and Packaging

Mention preferred colors, fabrics, woven labels, hang tags or custom packaging. These details can affect setup and unit costs.

Confirm Destination and Timing

All orders ship from China. Provide the destination country, city, postal code and deadline so suitable shipping channels can be considered. Standard production typically takes 7–10 days; larger orders may take longer. Shipping is quoted and timed separately.

Example Inquiry

Example: 200 navy six-panel baseball caps, white flat-embroidered front logo, logo file attached, individual packaging, delivery to the United States. Please quote production and international shipping separately and advise the estimated schedule.
