Why Quotes Vary

Custom hats are priced according to specifications, not just the number of units. Cap construction, fabric, colors, logo complexity, decoration technique, packaging and order quantity can all change the cost.

Choose Your Hat Style

Specify whether you need baseball caps, dad caps, trucker hats, bucket hats, golf caps or beanies. A reference photo can help when you do not know the style name.

Send Your Logo File

Attach an AI, EPS, SVG, PDF or high-resolution PNG file where available. Files can be submitted on the website, by email or via WhatsApp. Include the preferred logo size and front, side or back placement.

Specify Decoration and Quantity

Tell the team whether you prefer flat embroidery, 3D puff embroidery, printing or a patch. State the quantity you want quoted; you can also ask for comparisons at 50, 100, 500 or 1,000 units. Small-batch inquiries and samples are welcome.

Share Materials and Packaging

Mention preferred colors, fabrics, woven labels, hang tags or custom packaging. These details can affect setup and unit costs.

Confirm Destination and Timing

All orders ship from China. Provide the destination country, city, postal code and deadline so suitable shipping channels can be considered. Standard production typically takes around seven days; larger orders may take longer. Shipping is quoted and timed separately.

Example Inquiry

Example: 200 navy six-panel baseball caps, white flat-embroidered front logo, logo file attached, individual packaging, delivery to the United States. Please quote production and international shipping separately and advise the estimated schedule.

Practical advice for buyers

Write the enquiry as a specification

Include the cap style or reference photo, quantity by colour, fabric preference, closure, logo file and finished logo size. Say whether you need individual packaging, a sample and delivery to a specific country and postcode. Identify the date you actually need the stock.

Separate requirements from options

For example, request 200 navy caps with a front embroidered logo, then ask for a side patch as a separately priced option. State whether the fabric or closure can be changed to meet the target. This lets the supplier price a baseline and explain the trade-offs for each upgrade.

Review the returned quote line by line

Check the quantity split, logo locations, approval steps, sample and setup costs, packing and freight. Confirm when the price can change and when timing starts. Put decisions and revised artwork in a single thread so the quote and approval reference stay aligned.

Common buyer question

Can I ask for a quote without choosing a fabric?

Yes. Share the use case, preferred hand feel and budget range. Ask for two fabric options with their trade-offs and any effect on price.

How to Compare Two Custom Hat Quotes

A lower unit price only helps when both quotes cover the same finished hat. Compare the items below using the same quantity, color breakdown and delivery destination. List optional upgrades separately.

Quote itemWhat to confirm
Hat constructionPanel count, crown structure, brim shape, closure and finished measurements.
Logo decorationTechnique, dimensions, colors and every front, side or back location.
One-time costsSample development, embroidery digitizing, molds or other setup charges, where applicable.
PackagingInside labels, hang tags, individual packing and carton requirements.
DeliveryFreight service, destination, agreed delivery terms and which charges remain payable by the buyer.

Why did the price change after I sent my artwork?

A reference photo may hide details that affect production. A large embroidered area, additional logo positions or a custom patch can change the required work. Ask for an itemized revision showing the specification that changed before approving it.

Does a sample payment include bulk production?

Do not assume that it does. Confirm what the sample charge covers, whether shipping is separate, how revisions are handled and whether any credit toward a bulk order is agreed in writing.

How should I calculate the total cost per usable hat?

Add the quoted production amount, applicable setup and sample costs, packaging, freight and any destination charges you must pay. Divide by the quantity you expect to use. This is a budgeting calculation; ask for clarification of tax and import charges for your actual destination before comparing totals.

What if I need the hats for a fixed event date?

Give the required arrival date, not only the event date. Ask when artwork and sample approval must be completed, how the production schedule is confirmed and what allowance is needed for transport and customs. A production estimate does not include every delivery step.
