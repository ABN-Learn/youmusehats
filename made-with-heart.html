A Shared Standard from Sample to Production

Design, sales and production work from the same approved reference. Your cap profile, colour, logo placement and packaging requirements are documented so the details you approve at sampling can be checked again in production.

Care at Every Step

Materials That Suit the Hat

A cap is made from more than its outer fabric. We review how the crown fabric, brim covering, mesh, sweatband and closure work together. Compare the actual swatches if colour or hand feel matters, and agree which materials must match your approved sample.

From Cut Panels to Finished Stitching

Once the panels are cut, their alignment, seam finish and brim attachment shape the cap. Check the curve from the front and sides. For an embroidered logo, confirm its finished dimensions and placement, then inspect the fabric around the stitches for puckering.

Checks Before Packing

Use the approved sample and agreed tolerances to check the head opening, crown depth, closure and logo placement. When a check fails, record the actual measurement and affected units. Confirm the outcome of sorting, correction or reinspection before approving those units for packing.

Packing That Protects the Shape

Agree how caps will be nested, bagged and packed. Check that the carton protects the crown and brim, and verify that the colour, quantity and carton markings match the packing instructions.

Make the Design Your Own

Compare flat and raised embroidery, woven patches or print on the intended fabric. If your project includes a woven label, hang tag or branded packaging, review those details on the sample. Ask about close-up photos or video if you need to approve details remotely.
