Customized Service

Start with your design, quantity and intended use. YOUMUSE supports custom branding and OEM / ODM production, from fabric and fit choices to finished packaging.

  1. Share your brief. Send the quantity, cap colors, logo artwork, logo size, target head circumference and delivery destination.
  2. Choose construction and trim. Confirm fabric composition, weight, crown shape, sizing, decoration attachment and inside finishing.
  3. Review your sample. Approve the embroidered artwork, stitch coverage, fabric shade, fit and label positions before bulk production.
  4. Produce and inspect. Check construction, decoration alignment, fit, measurements and overall appearance against the approved sample.
  5. Pack and dispatch. Confirm packaging, carton details and the shipping method for your order.

Competitive Pricing

Factory quotations reflect your order quantity, fabric, stitch count and packaging. Send one complete brief so options can be compared on the same specification.

Strict Quality Control

Fabric and trim checks, in-process sewing checks and final inspection cover the details that affect fit, appearance and consistency.

Fast Turnaround

Typical production is around 7 days after sample approval; larger orders may require 15–20 days. Your confirmed schedule follows the final specification and quantity.

Packaging & Shipping

Protective packing

Choose individual polybags, tissue wrap, drawstring pouches, mailer boxes or rigid gift boxes. Pack the crown and brim with enough support to preserve the cap shape. Custom labels, hangtags and branded packaging can be included with your order.

Carton planning

Carton quantity, dimensions and gross weight are confirmed after the cap structure and packaging have been selected. A compact packing plan can reduce shipping volume while keeping the finished caps protected.

Shipping from China

Express and air shipping are available for samples, smaller orders and urgent deliveries. Selected international express services offer 3–5-day transit options, depending on destination and customs clearance. Sea freight can be arranged for larger shipments.

Provide the destination country, postal code, order quantity and required arrival date. We will confirm freight options, estimated transit time and the dispatch schedule with your quotation.
